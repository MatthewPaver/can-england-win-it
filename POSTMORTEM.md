# Time-boxed product close-out

**Archived:** 27 July 2026  
**Outcome:** The experiment shipped for a specific tournament moment and is now closed.

## What worked

- A playful three-step flow made a Monte Carlo model understandable without pretending it was a betting forecast.
- Seeded simulations, visible assumptions and analyst controls made the result inspectable.
- The project shipped as a responsive browser app with unit tests and an automated Pages build.

## Why this repository is archived

The product depended on a short-lived 2026 World Cup context. Once the referenced fixtures passed, an active live card became misleading and the maintenance value fell sharply. Archiving is more honest than quietly leaving a stale countdown or retrofitting an unrelated evergreen purpose.

## What the next time-boxed product should add

- an explicit expiry date in product metadata;
- an automated stale-content test for dates and fixture labels;
- a planned archive state and evergreen results page;
- real model inputs with provenance if the experience moves beyond an illustrative game.

The code remains public as a completed experiment. The model is illustrative, not financial or betting advice, and the repository receives no further feature development.
